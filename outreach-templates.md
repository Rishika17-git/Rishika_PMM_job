# Outreach Templates

Fill every `[bracket]`. Never send a template with a bracket still in it — specificity is the entire point; generic messages get ignored or reported as spam.
Rule of thumb: one message should take you 5-10 min to personalize, referencing something real about their product/launch.

---

## 1. LinkedIn connection note — to a senior PMM/marketing lead (they may or may not be hiring)

> Hi [Name] — saw [Company]'s [specific launch/feature/campaign, e.g. "Product Hunt launch last week" or "the repositioning around X"]. I'm a self-taught product marketer (media background, ~[X] yrs solo PMM experience) actively looking to work under an experienced PMM to sharpen fundamentals I've had to figure out alone. Not fishing for a generic intro — genuinely curious how you're thinking about [specific thing, e.g. "positioning for the launch"]. Open to a 15-min chat?

## 2. Follow-up after they accept / reply positively

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! Quick context on me: [1-2 lines from brief — biggest concrete result, e.g. "led GTM for X launch that did Y"]. I'd love 15 min to hear how PMM works at [Company] — and honestly, if there's ever room for an associate-level PMM on your team, I'd want to be first in line. No pressure either way, just want to learn from someone who's done this longer than I have.

## 3. Cold application via Wellfound / YC Work at a Startup (founder-facing, no formal posting yet)

> Hi [Founder/Hiring Manager name] — [Company] caught my eye because [specific reason: stage, product, market]. I've been doing product marketing solo for [X] at [previous org] — [one concrete outcome]. I'm looking for my next role specifically at a place with an experienced PMM/marketing lead already in seat, because I want structured mentorship while I execute, not to rebuild the function alone again. If you're building out that layer of the team, I'd love to talk.

## 4. Reply to an actual open PMM/associate PMM posting

> Applying for [Role] at [Company]. Quick summary: [2 lines — background, standout result, why product marketing specifically vs. broader marketing]. What drew me to this role specifically: [Company]'s stage + the fact that [Name/title] already leads PMM here — I'm looking for exactly that kind of mentorship alongside real ownership. Resume attached; happy to walk through [specific relevant project] on a call.

## 5. Informational-interview ask to a PMM leader NOT at a hiring company (reverse-engineering channel)

> Hi [Name] — I've been following your [talk/podcast/post] on [specific topic, e.g. "narrative-driven positioning"]. I'm early-career in product marketing (self-taught, media background) and trying to learn from people doing it well rather than guessing alone like I did at my last role. Would you have 15 min for a couple of questions? Not asking for a job — just perspective from someone more senior in the discipline.
> *(Close if it lands well: "If you ever hear of teams looking for an associate-level PMM who wants to learn fast, I'd be grateful for a nudge.")*

## 6. Polite bump (day 5-7 of silence, one only)

> Hi [Name] — know things get busy, just floating this back up. Still very interested in [Company/the chat] — no worries if the timing's off, just didn't want it to get buried.

---

## Notes on tone
- Never say "just checking in" with nothing new to add — always attach a fresh, small piece of context if bumping.
- Lead with something specific about *them* before talking about yourself.
- State the mentorship angle plainly — it's a differentiator, not something to hide. Founders/PMM leads read "I want to learn from you" as low-ego and high-growth, not weak.
- Keep every message under ~100 words. Long cold messages don't get read.
