# PMM Job Search Strategy — Target: Offer by Aug 10, 2026

**Start date:** Jul 21, 2026 · **Deadline:** Aug 10, 2026 (~20 days / 3 weeks)
**Role target:** Associate/mid-level Product Marketing, at a startup that already has a senior/experienced PMM in place (mentor + execution, not solo-PMM ownership).

## Why this shape of role
- Solo PMM roles = no mentorship (the exact problem at the last job). Excluded from search.
- Trainee/intern/executive-only roles = too junior, no real ownership. Excluded.
- Target: a startup (ideally global, growth/launch-stage) where a senior PMM or Head of PMM already exists and is hiring a #2.

## Weekly plan

### Week 1 (Jul 21–27) — Build + first wave
- Finalize personal brief (see `brief-template.md`) — needs your resume, paste it in and I'll tailor it.
- Set up profiles/alerts on all 5 platforms above; save searches filtered to "Product Marketing" + Series A/B or "growth stage."
- Build target list in `tracker.csv`: aim for 25 companies with a confirmed senior PMM/Head of Marketing already on the team.
- Send first 15 outreach messages (mix of informational-interview asks to senior PMMs + direct applications where a role is open).
- Join PMA + Sharebird communities, introduce yourself, engage on 2-3 threads (visibility before asking).

### Week 2 (Jul 28–Aug 3) — Scale + convert
- 15-20 more outreach sends; follow up on Week 1's silence (one polite bump at day 5-7, not before).
- Prioritize any replies immediately — informational chats this week can convert to referrals fast given the timeline.
- Start interview prep in parallel: PMM case-study/positioning exercise practice, since growth-stage startups almost always test this.

### Week 3 (Aug 4–10) — Close
- Focus all energy on live conversations/interviews, not new outreach.
- Use the mentor-fit filter explicitly in interviews: ask who you'd report to, how long they've been in PMM, what ramp-up/mentorship looks like.
- Negotiate and close.

## Daily rhythm (repeatable, not automated)
1. Check saved searches on the 5 platforms (10 min).
2. Add any new qualifying companies to `tracker.csv`.
3. Send that day's outreach batch (3-5/day sustainable pace beats a burst).
4. Log responses same day.

## What I (Claude) do vs. what only you can do
- **I can:** research companies/programs, draft and refine messages, track status, prep interview positioning, keep this plan current.
- **Only you can:** actually browse/connect on LinkedIn, click send, attend interviews, negotiate. I'm not able to automate LinkedIn interaction — see channel #6 above.
