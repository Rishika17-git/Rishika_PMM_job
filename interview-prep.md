# PMM Interview Prep

Aug 10 deadline means interviews likely land in Week 2-3 with almost no runway to prep after the invite comes in. Build this now, before you need it.

## What PMM interviews actually test
Not technical depth — your thought process on customers, positioning, and driving adoption through influence (not authority). Almost every process ends in some form of case study: build a GTM plan, segment an audience, or critique/improve a launch.

## Answer structure for any case study (use every time)
1. **Situation** — product/feature, business context, why it matters now.
2. **Audience** — who specifically, and why them first.
3. **Positioning** — the one sentence a customer would use to describe why this beats alternatives.
4. **Plan** — channels, sequencing, cross-functional dependencies (sales enablement, product, CS).
5. **Metrics** — what "working" looks like, tied to adoption/revenue/win-rate, not vanity metrics.
6. **Result** (for past-experience questions) — quantify. If you don't have a hard number, use a directional one and say so honestly.

## Prep this week (do it before any interview is scheduled, not after)
- [ ] Pick 3 of your own past launches/projects and rewrite them in the 6-step structure above — memorize the shape, not a script.
- [ ] Pick one product you don't work on (ideally at a target company) and do a cold case study on it: audience, positioning, plan, metrics. This is exactly what they'll ask you to do live.
- [ ] Prepare your honest narrative on "why solo PMM → why you want a senior PMM now" — this will come up, and framing it as intentional (not a gap) is a strength, not something to apologize for.

## Questions to ask them (these do double duty — screen for mentor-fit AND signal seriousness)
- "Who would I report to, and how long have they been doing PMM specifically?"
- "What does ramp-up/onboarding look like for this role in the first 90 days?"
- "What's an example of something the PMM lead here has mentored someone through recently?"
- "How is work split between you and the rest of the marketing/PMM team day to day?"
- If answers reveal you'd be the only PMM or the "senior PMM" is actually a generalist marketing head with no PMM background — that's a signal to walk, even under deadline pressure. A wrong-fit fast hire recreates the original problem.
